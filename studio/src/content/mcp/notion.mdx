## Overview

The official Notion MCP server lets Claude interact with your Notion workspace. Read pages, create new content, query databases, and keep your knowledge base up to date automatically.

## Use Cases

- **Meeting Notes**: Auto-generate and save meeting summaries to Notion
- **Content Planning**: Manage editorial calendars and content databases
- **Knowledge Base**: Search and retrieve information from your Notion docs
- **Task Management**: Create and update tasks in Notion databases
