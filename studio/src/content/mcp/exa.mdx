## Overview

Exa Search MCP provides Claude with semantic web search — finding content based on meaning rather than just keyword matching. Results are more relevant for research and technical queries.

## Use Cases

- **Technical Research**: Find semantically relevant technical articles
- **Competitive Analysis**: Research competitors and industry trends
- **Literature Review**: Find relevant papers and articles
- **Content Research**: Gather comprehensive background information
