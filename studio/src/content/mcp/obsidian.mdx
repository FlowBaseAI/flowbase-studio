## Overview

Obsidian MCP connects Claude to your Obsidian vault, enabling reading, writing, and searching through your notes and knowledge base.

## Use Cases

- **Note Summarization**: Summarize vault notes on demand
- **Knowledge Retrieval**: Search and retrieve related notes
- **Content Creation**: Write new notes based on your existing knowledge
- **Zettelkasten**: Help build connections between notes
