# Content Writing Rules

## Writing Style

- Active voice preferred over passive
- Present tense for instructions and descriptions
- Second person ("you") to address the reader
- Sentences under 25 words on average
- One idea per paragraph
- Short paragraphs — 3-4 sentences maximum

## Clarity

- Lead with the most important information (inverted pyramid)
- Define jargon on first use or avoid it
- Use specific numbers over vague claims ("3x faster" not "much faster")
- Concrete examples over abstract explanations
- Avoid filler phrases: "In order to" → "To", "Due to the fact that" → "Because"

## Structure

- Every piece needs a hook in the first sentence
- Subheadings every 300-400 words in long-form content
- Bullet points for 3+ parallel items
- Bold for key terms and important callouts (not decoration)
- End with clear next step or CTA

## Tone Guidelines

- Professional but not corporate
- Confident but not arrogant
- Helpful but not condescending
- Conversational but not informal

## Forbidden

- "Synergy", "leverage" (as verb), "circle back", "bandwidth" (non-technical)
- "Very", "really", "quite", "somewhat" (weak modifiers)
- "Obviously", "clearly", "as everyone knows" (condescending)
- Excessive exclamation marks
- Starting sentences with "So,"

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Hook in first sentence
- [ ] Clear structure with headings
- [ ] Every claim supported or qualified
- [ ] Specific CTA at end
- [ ] Read aloud test (if it sounds awkward, rewrite)
