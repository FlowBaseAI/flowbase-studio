## Overview

Google Drive MCP lets Claude read and search your Google Drive files including Docs, Sheets, and Slides. Claude can analyze documents, extract data from spreadsheets, and summarize files.

## Use Cases

- **Document Analysis**: Analyze and summarize Google Docs
- **Spreadsheet Insights**: Extract insights from Google Sheets
- **File Search**: Find files by content across your Drive
- **Report Generation**: Generate reports based on Drive data
